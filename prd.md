# JeevanSetu Product Requirements Document

## Product
JeevanSetu is an AI-powered rural healthcare access, assistance, coordination and service-monitoring platform for rural and underserved communities.

## Core problem
Patients struggle to find relevant healthcare resources, complete referrals, discover schemes and assistance, and access services without smartphones. PHCs can face medicine-stock shortages and limited visibility into service activity.

## Primary users
- Patient
- PHC staff
- Doctor
- Hospital
- NGO
- Admin

## Primary features
1. Patient healthcare case
2. Verified hospitals, schemes and NGOs
3. AI-assisted resource recommendation
4. Referral creation and follow-up tracking
5. PHC medicine inventory
6. Medicine depletion warning/prediction
7. PHC service activity monitoring
8. Admin alerts and analytics

## Secondary features
- AI healthcare assistant
- IVR/local-language access
- Privacy-preserving feedback
- Organ donation information
- Feedback classification
- Future outbreak early warning

## AI scope
AI may assist with resource recommendation, controlled conversation, document information extraction and feedback classification.

ML may handle medicine forecasting, service anomaly detection and future outbreak-risk modelling.

## Safety boundaries
JeevanSetu is not a diagnostic or prescribing system. AI must not diagnose, prescribe, guarantee eligibility, fabricate resources, or accuse healthcare workers. Automated service anomalies are flags for human review.

## MVP
Authentication, roles, patient case, verified resource directory, AI recommendation, referral tracking, PHC inventory, basic medicine prediction, service monitoring and admin alerts.

## Success criteria
A patient can securely create a case, find verified resources, receive grounded recommendations, create and track a referral, and a PHC/admin can manage inventory and review alerts.

## Product principle
Keep every feature connected to improving rural/underserved healthcare access, coordination or public-health service quality.
