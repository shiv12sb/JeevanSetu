Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

Annual Coverage Benefit:₹5,00,000 per family per year

Eligibility: Families identified in SECC 2011 rural database, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) ration cardholders.

Covers: Secondary and tertiary hospitalizations, cardiac procedures, oncology, trauma surgery.

Docs:Aadhaar CardRation Card (BPL/AAY)Ayushman Card or Mobile Number
Available directly at all empaneled PHCs, Civil Hospital Ayushman Mitra desks, or online at NHA portal.
Official Govt Portal
State Universal Health SchemeVerified Govt SchemeHelpline: 155388 / 18002332200

Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)

Maharashtra State Health Assurance Scheme

Annual Coverage Benefit:₹5,00,000 per family per year

Eligibility: All families holding Yellow, Orange, or White Ration Cards in Maharashtra.

Covers: 996 medical and surgical procedures across 30 identified specialties.

Docs:Aadhaar CardRation CardDoctor Referral Note
Contact Aarogyamitra at any verified government or empaneled private hospital, or visit Jeevandayee portal.
Official Govt Portal
Maternal & Child HealthVerified Govt SchemeHelpline: 102

Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)

Maternal and Neonatal Health Protection Scheme

Annual Coverage Benefit:100% Free institutional delivery, medicines, food and transport

Eligibility: All pregnant women delivering in public health institutions and sick newborns up to 1 year.

Covers: Free C-section, blood transfusion, diagnostics, and free transport from home to PHC/hospital.

Docs:Mother-Child Protection (MCP) CardAadhaar Card
Automatically activated at any PHC, Sub-District Hospital, or District Civil Hospital.
Official Govt Portal

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Direct Benefit Transfer for Maternal Nutrition

Annual Coverage Benefit:₹5,000 to ₹6,000 Direct Cash Benefit into Bank Account

Eligibility: Pregnant women and lactating mothers for first living child (and second child if female).

Covers: Wage-loss compensation, antenatal care registration, and infant immunization incentives.

Docs:Aadhaar Card (Mother & Husband)MCP CardBank Account linked with Aadhaar
Register via local ASHA/Anganwadi worker or apply online on PMMVY official portal.
Official Govt Portal

Nikshay (National TB Elimination Programme)

Direct Benefit Transfer & Free TB Treatment Scheme

Annual Coverage Benefit:100% Free Diagnostic Tests, Anti-TB Drugs & ₹500/month Nutrition Support

Eligibility: All citizens diagnosed with Tuberculosis across India.

Covers: Free CBNAAT/TrueNat testing, complete course of antitubercular medications, and direct nutritional cash support.

Docs:Aadhaar CardBank PassbookSputum Test Report from PHC
Enrolled automatically at any Government PHC or directly at Nikshay Web Portal.
