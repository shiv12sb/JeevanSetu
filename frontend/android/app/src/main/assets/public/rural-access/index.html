Rural Health Access (ग्रामीण आरोग्य पोहोच)

Equitable last-mile healthcare delivery for citizens with basic 2G keypad phones without internet. Dispatches automated voice guidance and queues door-to-door ASHA home visits.

Phase-2 Deployment Scope & Statutory Telecommunications Framework

Prototype Evaluation Mode

• Statutory Telecom Regulation: Under the Indian Telegraph Act (1885) and TRAI TCCCPR Regulations, broadcasting live automated outbound calls from a Toll-Free 1800 number to commercial GSM cellular handsets requires institutional DLT Principal Entity Registration and dedicated PRI/SIP trunk procurement via licensed Telecom Service Providers (Tata Tele, Airtel, BSNL).

• Future Statewide Activation Roadmap: In this prototype stage, outbound calls execute via standard SIP telephony webhooks. Upon post-empanelment grant funding under the National Health Mission (NHM) Maharashtra, dedicated enterprise telephony gateways (Exotel / Twilio / Tata Tele) will be activated for full cellular PSTN dialing across all 36 districts.

JeevanSetu Village Health Access Network

JeevanSetu acts as an information routing and care-coordination layer connecting patients from remote tribal hamlets to regional apex hospitals:

Level 1

Remote Citizen / Keypad Phone

Receives automated Toll-Free call (1800-108-102) in Marathi and requests ASHA home visit.

➔
Level 2

ASHA Frontline Worker

Sees incoming ticket in live queue, calls resident back, and visits home with medical kit.

➔
Level 3

Primary Health Centre (PHC)

Initiates digital referral pathways to specialized hospitals and checks anti-venom/blood stock.

➔
Level 4

District Trauma Hospital

Admits referred patient immediately under pre-verified government schemes (PMJAY/MJPJAY).
